### Executive Summary: AI & Design Research (August 9, 2026)

*   **OpenAI GPT-5.6 Deployment:** OpenAI has fully rolled out the GPT-5.6 family, introducing the "Sol" model with a customizable reasoning-effort slider for Plus/Pro users and making the "Luna" model the new standard for Free users, now featuring unlimited text chats and a "Think" button.
