### Executive Summary: AI & Design Research (October 21, 2026)

*   **Meta Launches Muse Glimmer 30B:** Meta has introduced an open-source, 30-billion parameter model optimized for local, on-device deployment. It is specifically engineered to handle autonomous agentic tasks, featuring advanced multi-step reasoning and integrated tool-use capabilities.
*   **Expansion of On-Device Autonomy:** The release of Muse Glimmer 30B signals a major shift toward privacy-focused, decentralized AI, allowing developers to execute complex workflows directly on hardware without relying on cloud-based processing.
*   **Financial Cents MCP Integration:** Financial Cents has launched a new Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, bridging the gap between proprietary accounting data and generative AI.
