# AI & Design Daily Digest — 2026-09-28

## Executive Summary

### Executive Summary: AI & Design Developments (September 28, 2026)

*   **Scalable Generative Design:** Figma launched "Weave tools," enabling designers to build and publish custom, scalable generative AI workflows for image and vector assets directly within their design files, significantly streamlining production pipelines.
*   **Agentic GUI Interaction:** H Company released "Holo4," a series of open-weight computer-use models (27B and 35B) specifically engineered to autonomously navigate GUIs, execute code, and operate MCP tools to complete complex multi-step tasks.
*   **Coherent Long-Form Video:** Google Research introduced an agentic "AI Video Co-Director" framework that treats long-form video production as a global optimization problem, allowing for the generation of coherent, minutes-long cinematic content through advanced world-state tracking.
*   **Vertical AI Integration:** Physitrack launched an agentic EMR system for rehabilitation clinics, utilizing specialized AI to automate clinical documentation and suggest personalized exercise programs, marking a shift toward autonomous administrative support in healthcare.
*   **Enterprise Agent Security:** To address the risks of autonomous systems, Nvidia launched the "Open Agent Safety Platform," providing critical sandboxing and independent security controls to prevent agents from executing unauthorized actions or drifting from designated operational parameters.
