### Executive Summary: AI Agent Governance (September 2026)

*   **Emergence of the Governance Stack:** The industry has shifted toward formalizing "Agent Governance," with major enterprise players launching specialized infrastructure to manage, track, and secure autonomous AI agents in real-time.
*   **Broadcom AgentMinder Launch:** Broadcom introduced AgentMinder, an autonomous "traffic controller" that mandates real-time identity verification and granular authorization for AI agents attempting to access restricted enterprise resources.
*   **IBM AgentOps Capabilities:** IBM expanded its watsonx Orchestrate suite with the AgentOps Agent, providing a dedicated control plane that enables step-by-step execution path tracing and custom evaluation systems for complex AI workflows.
*   **Strategic Shift Toward Accountability:** These developments reflect a concentrated effort to mitigate AI risks, moving from experimental agent deployment to controlled, observable systems designed for enterprise-grade transparency and security.
