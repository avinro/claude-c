### Executive Summary: AI & Design Research (October 20, 2026)

*   **Design-to-Code Parity:** Figma has significantly accelerated the design-to-development workflow with the public beta launch of "Code Layers," enabling direct translation of interactive prototypes into functional code, while the new Markdown-based "Specs" editor ensures AI agents adhere to strict brand guidelines.
*   **Enhanced AI Agent Personalization:** Framer introduced its "Skills" feature, allowing teams to curate and reuse specific instructions for design systems and CMS workflows, drastically improving the precision and consistency of Framer’s AI agent outputs.
