### Executive Summary: AI & Design Research (July 28, 2026)

*   **Standardized Protocol Efficiency:** The newly finalized Model Context Protocol (MCP) stateless specification eliminates the need for initialize handshakes and session IDs, significantly streamlining infrastructure requirements for AI integrations.
*   **Enterprise Agentic Governance:** Onspring has integrated Anthropic’s Claude into its GRC platform, enabling administrators to deploy governed, autonomous workflows that ensure AI-driven tasks remain within corporate compliance boundaries.
*   **Expansion of No-Code AI Orchestration:** 7AI launched "7AI Build," a platform extension that allows enterprises to architect custom agentic workflows, specialized skill sets, and embedded security services directly within the 7AI environment.
*   **Advanced Model Evaluation:** Council 1.2 has introduced a "guest seat" feature, enhancing design research workflows by allowing users to import external AI outputs into a blind-review environment for collaborative, anonymous cross-model critique.
