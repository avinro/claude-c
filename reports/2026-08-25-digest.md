## Executive Summary

### Executive Summary: AI & Design Research (August 25, 2026)

*   **Figma Expands Agent Capabilities:** Figma has updated its design agent with new skill sets and Model Context Protocol (MCP) support, enabling designers to natively create, share, and remix custom agent workflows directly within the design environment.
*   **Enterprise Automation via MCP:** ZINFI Technologies has launched an early-access MCP server, allowing enterprise AI agents to securely query and execute actions across partner onboarding, deal registration, and incentive management systems.
*   **Advancements in World Modeling:** Two key research breakthroughs enhance AI simulation accuracy: *ReWorld* introduces long-horizon memory for complex task environments, while new research on physical invariants significantly improves prediction accuracy in world-model rollouts.
*   **Specialized AI Workflows:** Wórkiro has launched an AI-powered automation module tailored for accounting firms, featuring automated task routing and AI-generated activity summaries to streamline client jobs and MTD compliance.
*   **Self-Improvement & Cognitive Research:** The *Prime Agent* framework provides a new harness for self-improving Reinforcement Learning Models (RLM), while concurrent academic research is investigating the long-term impact of AI assistance on human cognitive development and skill acquisition.
