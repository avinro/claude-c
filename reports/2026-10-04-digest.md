## Executive Summary

### Executive Summary: AI & Design Research (October 2026)

*   **Aleph Alpha Launches Kolibri:** The company released "Kolibri," an open-weight, 78B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) transformer model. Designed for English-German bilingual support, it offers a high-capacity context window of 1 million tokens.
*   **Framer Integrates "Skills":** Framer updated its AI agent with "Skills," a new feature enabling designers to train the AI on proprietary design systems, specific brand voices, and CMS workflows.
*   **Enhanced Design Scalability:** The introduction of Framer Skills significantly improves cross-project consistency, allowing teams to automate repetitive tasks by reusing specialized workflows taught to the AI.
*   **Expansion of Open-Weights Ecosystem:** The Kolibri release provides enterprise-grade, large-context capabilities for developers, signaling a continued shift toward high-performance, open-weight models for complex linguistic tasks.
